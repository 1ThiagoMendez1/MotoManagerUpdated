# Public privacy and data deletion notices

Canonical public URLs:

- https://motomanager.com.co/privacidad
- https://motomanager.com.co/eliminacion-de-datos

The notices identify Mivra S.A.S. and info@mivra.com.co, distinguish the
platform's account relationship from data administered by workshops, describe
operational and optional promotional messages, and cover Wompi and the Meta
app Mivra's WhatsApp Business integration. They disclose authorized history,
contacts, mobile-app echoes, tokens and message state; pausing/revoking the
integration is distinguished from requesting data deletion. Requests are
handled by email, without promising an automated deletion feature.

Facts checked against the trial form, advertising preferences, anonymous
collector and current coexistence implementation. Identity, response periods
and rights use Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012, especially articles 8, 14–16:
https://cancilleria.gov.co/normograma/compilacion/docs/ley_1581_2012.htm

Effective date is 12 November 2026 in America/Bogota. No unverified NIT,
retention duration, certification, service address or approval claim is added.

Both documents are static HTML route responses. They do not mount the app
layout, analytics or Meta SDK, and the two exact paths bypass session redirects
so they remain readable even with expired or restricted sessions. Their CSP
disables scripts and external resources; all styling and branding are local.
Footer and registration links make the policy discoverable. No database,
connection mode, credentials or message delivery configuration changes.

Public delivery also needs to preserve contact addresses without JavaScript.
The body uses Cloudflare's documented `email_off` HTML markers so its email
obfuscation does not hide the privacy contact or inject a decode script:
https://developers.cloudflare.com/waf/tools/scrape-shield/email-address-obfuscation/
